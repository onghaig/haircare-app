Scanning

My results jump around between scans

Lighting is the usual cause. Scan in the same room, facing a window or a bright light, with dry hair and no product. Follow the laser line until each angle locks. The app rejects shots it can't read, and one scan is never treated as a trend.

The scan won't lock on an angle

Move somewhere brighter, hold the phone at arm's length, and pull hair off your forehead. For the top and crown, a second person or a mirror helps. If it still won't lock, email us with your phone model.

How often should I scan?

Every two to four weeks is plenty. Hair changes slowly, and the app reminds you when a new scan is due.

Why is my estimate a range and not one number?

Because photos and forecasts have error. A range is the honest answer. It narrows as you add scans and family history.

Subscriptions and refunds

Cancel a subscription

  • iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Fuller, Cancel Subscription. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play, tap your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Fuller, Cancel. Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel your subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Ask for a refund

  • iPhone: request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: request it from Google Play in your order history, or see Google's refund help.

We can't issue refunds ourselves, because we never see your payment details.

Restore a purchase

New phone or reinstalled? Open the app, go to the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Delete your data

Fuller doesn't have accounts, and your scans, photos, scores, forecasts and logs are stored only on your phone. That means you can delete them yourself:

  • One scan: open the scan and tap Delete this scan. Its photos, measurements and any AI previews made from it are removed straight away.
  • AI previews: turn AI previews off on the AI preview screen. Every preview is deleted from the phone.
  • Everything: delete the Fuller app. All of its data on your phone goes with it. If your phone backs up to iCloud or Google, old backups may still contain app data until they're replaced.

We don't keep a copy of any of it. AI preview photos are only held in memory while the image is made. If you joined the waitlist or emailed us and want that removed, email [support email] from the same address and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Medical questions

We can't give medical advice or tell you which treatment to use. Fuller's numbers are estimates for tracking. For treatment, talk to a doctor, dermatologist or licensed telehealth provider. If you have sudden or patchy hair loss, scalp pain, or other symptoms, see a doctor.

If you're having side effects from a medicine, stop and contact your prescriber. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
