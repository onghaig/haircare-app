The short version

  • Your scans stay on your phone. Photos of your head, your Scalp Map, scores, forecasts, answers and daily logs are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We don't have a copy.
  • No account. You don't sign up, and there's no Fuller profile on a server.
  • One exception, and it's opt in. If you're 18 or older and turn on AI previews, one front photo is sent to make the image. It isn't stored by us.
  • No ads, no selling, no tracking. We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or put health information into analytics tools.
  • Delete any time. Delete a scan in the app and its photos go with it. Delete the app and everything on it is gone.

What stays on your phone

Fuller is built so the sensitive parts never need to reach us. The following is created and kept only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Scan photos. The five photos from each Fuller Scan (front, both temples, top and crown), and the Scalp Map made from them.
  • Measurements and results. Your Hair Score, Norwood estimate, hair quality readings, Two Futures forecast and progress over time.
  • Your answers. Age, sex, where you notice changes, and your family's hair history.
  • Family photos. Photos of relatives you choose to add to your Family Forecast. Only add photos of people who are happy for you to.
  • Your plan and daily log. Treatments you track, reminders, scalp massage, sleep, shedding and other notes.
  • Old photos you import to fill in your timeline.

The scan uses points on your face (like the corners of your eyes) to line each photo up with the last. That happens on the phone. We don't create a face template to identify you, and nothing is sent to us.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They never name a medicine on the lock screen.

Before launch, confirm the product scanner, Try-On and transplant preview also run on the phone. If any of them send data off the device, add them to the next section.

What leaves your phone

AI previews (optional, 18+)

Fuller can show a drawn illustration of your forecast, made on your phone. If you're 18 or older you can also choose an AI preview, which redraws your hair on your own photo. It's off until you agree on a screen that explains it, and it's never offered to anyone under 18.

When you ask for a preview, the app sends:

  • your latest front scan photo, shrunk to 768 pixels wide, and
  • two numbers: the forecast's most likely Norwood stage and the number of years ahead.

These go to our preview service, which runs on Cloudflare. It passes them to Google's Gemini image model and sends the result back to your phone. Our service keeps the photo in memory for that one request only. It doesn't store or log it. The finished image is saved only on your phone. Turning AI previews off deletes every preview from the device.

Google processes the photo under its paid Gemini API terms, which say Google doesn't use it to improve its products. Google may keep request data for a limited time to detect abuse, as described in its terms.

Lawyer to check: the Google retention wording against the current Gemini API terms, and whether this needs separate consent wording for biometric or consumer-health laws (for example Illinois, Texas, Washington).

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle payment, and we never see your card details. The app receives confirmation of what you bought and when it renews or ends, so it can turn features on.

When you contact us

If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write. Don't send us scan photos unless we ask and you're comfortable with it.

Crash reports and analytics

The app doesn't include analytics or advertising tools today. If we add crash reporting or usage stats, we'll update this policy first, keep them free of health information, and tell you in the app.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • AI preview photos: not kept by us. Only held in memory while the image is made.
  • Waitlist emails: until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

Because your scans and logs live on your phone, you're in control of them directly:

  • Delete a scan in the app to remove its photos, measurements and any AI previews made from it.
  • Turn AI previews off in the app to delete every preview.
  • Delete the app to remove everything Fuller stored on your device. Check your phone's backups too, since iCloud or Google backups can include app data.

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU, California and other US states), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or move personal data we hold, to object or withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email [support email] and we'll reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Health data

Hair, scalp and treatment information can count as health data. We treat it that way even though it stays on your phone: it's never used for ads, never put into analytics, and never sold. The only health-related data that leaves your phone is the AI preview request described above, and only with your consent.

Fuller gives estimates for tracking your hair. It's not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything.

Children and teens

Fuller is for people 13 and older. If you're under 18, the app doesn't show prescription options and doesn't offer AI previews. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Lawyer to check: some EU countries set the age of digital consent at 14 to 16.

Security

Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's own security. Connections to our preview service use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and up to date.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where UK or EU law applies, we rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses for those transfers.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data leaves your phone, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
