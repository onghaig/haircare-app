1. Not medical advice

Fuller is a wellness and tracking app. Its Norwood estimates, Hair Score, quality readings, Two Futures forecasts, treatment comparisons, try-on suggestions and transplant previews are estimates, shown as ranges, based on photos taken in everyday conditions and on statistical models.

Fuller is not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, and it doesn't replace a doctor, dermatologist or other licensed professional. Information about treatments is general information to help you talk to a professional, not a recommendation or a prescription. Only a licensed clinician can decide whether a treatment suits you. Never ignore or delay medical advice because of something you saw in Fuller.

Results vary. We don't promise that your hair will stay, grow back or look any particular way.
